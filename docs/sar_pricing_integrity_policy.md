# Royal Umrah Cabs — Authoritative SAR Pricing Integrity Policy

## 1. Primary Currency Mandate
- **Authoritative Business Currency:** All commercial rates, fare calculations, internal bookings, driver dispatch orders, and customer receipts are strictly denominated in **Saudi Riyal (SAR)**.
- **Zero Currency Ambiguity:** The customer pays the agreed amount in SAR upon arrival, or via authorized payment gateway in SAR equivalent. No fluctuating exchange rates are applied once a booking confirmation code is issued.
