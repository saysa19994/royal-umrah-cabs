# Fleet Visual Asset Specifications & Generation Briefs
**Project:** Royal Umrah Cabs / Duyuf Alrahman VIP (`duyufalrahmanvip.com`)  
**Art Direction:** Senior Automotive Photography Art Director & Fleet Verification Team

---

## 1. Photography & Asset Guidelines

To preserve executive credibility and customer trust:
1. **Model Accuracy:** Only depict actual verified vehicle generations (2024–2025). No generic or inaccurate body kits.
2. **Livery & Appearance:** Pearl white or deep onyx black with VIP privacy tinted glass. Pristine showroom condition.
3. **Environment:** Real Saudi architectural contexts (Makkah luxury hotel drop-offs, Jeddah King Abdulaziz International Airport Terminal 1 arrivals, Madinah palm avenues). Soft golden-hour ambient lighting.
4. **Resolution & Optimization:** WebP format, high visual acuity, compressed for sub-second mobile page loads with explicit `width` and `height` to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
