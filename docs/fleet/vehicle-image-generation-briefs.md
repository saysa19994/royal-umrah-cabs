## 1. Photography & Asset Guidelines

To preserve executive credibility, customer trust, and VIP branding:
1. **Model Accuracy:** Only depict actual verified vehicle models (2024–2025). No inaccurate trims, incorrect badges, or mismatched models.
2. **Livery & Appearance:** Pearl white, obsidian black, or executive metallic finishes with VIP privacy tinted glass. Pristine showroom condition.
3. **Environment:** Real Saudi architectural contexts (Makkah luxury hotel porte-cochères, Jeddah King Abdulaziz International Airport Terminal 1 arrivals, Madinah palm avenues). Soft golden-hour ambient lighting and reflections.
4. **Resolution & Optimization:** Standardized high-resolution 1920x1080 master assets, optimized JPEG and WebP formats, explicit aspect ratios to eliminate Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
5. **The 5 Mandatory Visual Angles:**
   - **Angle 1:** Exterior Front (3/4 front view showcasing grille, LED headlamps, and road stance)
   - **Angle 2:** Exterior Back/Rear (Rear 3/4 view showcasing taillights, liftgate, and luggage area access)
   - **Angle 3:** Side Profile (Full lateral elevation showcasing wheelbase, tinted windows, and wheels)
   - **Angle 4:** Interior Seats/Legroom (Executive passenger cabin, leather upholstery, legroom, and comfort amenities)
   - **Angle 5:** Interior Dashboard (Driver cockpit, multifunction steering wheel, digital displays, and dual climate controls)
