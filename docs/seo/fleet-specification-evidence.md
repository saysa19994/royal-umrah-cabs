## 1. Context & Discrepancy Analysis

The previous report referenced: **21 passengers and 20 large luggage items** for the Toyota Coaster VIP.  
However, the official published specification by **Abdul Latif Jameel Motors (Toyota Saudi Arabia / ALJ)** lists:
- **Toyota Coaster 2.8L Diesel 20-Seater:** 20 seats **including the driver** (19 passenger seats + 1 chauffeur).
- **Toyota Coaster 2.8L Diesel 23-Seater:** 23 seats **including the driver** (22 passenger seats + 1 chauffeur).

In standard factory configuration, a 20-seat Coaster does **not** possess a factory luggage compartment capable of accommodating 20 large (28-inch) hard-shell suitcases without occupying passenger seats or undergoing an authorized interior conversion.
