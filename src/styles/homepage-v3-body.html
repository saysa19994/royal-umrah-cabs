Frequently asked questions

Answers before
you travel.

Clear information about fares, airport pickups, payment and your journey.

How do I book a private transfer?

Choose your pickup, destination, travel date and vehicle, then submit your contact details. Your request is reviewed and confirmed by the team.

When do I pay for my journey?

Payment is made in cash directly to your driver after the journey is completed. You can pay in SAR or USD. No advance payment, online payment or POS payment is required.

Do you monitor flight arrivals?

Yes. Share your airline and flight number when booking an airport pickup so the team can monitor your arrival and coordinate the pickup.

How much airport waiting time is included?

60 minutes of complimentary airport waiting are included. International arrival bookings may have a 60–90 minute waiting allowance, subject to the applicable booking terms.

Can I request a child seat?

Child seats are available on request. Please mention your requirement when submitting the booking request.

Can we stop at the Miqat on the way?

A complimentary Miqat stop of approximately 30–45 minutes is available on relevant journeys. Please mention the stop when arranging your trip.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before the scheduled journey.

Which destinations do you serve?

Private transportation is available on selected routes serving Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Taif and Riyadh.

Which vehicle should I choose?

Choose from Toyota Camry, Hyundai Staria, GMC Yukon XL, Toyota HiAce and Toyota Coaster. The suitable option depends on your passenger count, luggage and route. Confirm the exact capacity before booking.
